Azan Clock

Privacy and credits

Windows app

Settings, saved prayer schedules and the optional prayer log are stored on your PC. The app contains no advertising or download tracking. Windows location is requested when you click Get my location. Location lookups use Photon, manual city searches use Open-Meteo, and AlAdhan receives location information to return prayer times.

Website and download links

GitHub hosts the site and files and reports aggregate downloads per file. Different share links point to byte-identical EXEs with distinct file names so we can see which channel led to downloads.

When country tracking is active, Cloudflare estimates the country from the connection when a visitor starts a download on the official site. The counter stores daily totals by country and share channel. It stores no IP addresses, cookies or individual download histories.
