Daily prayer times

Choose a city in Japan or worldwide. Maghrib and the other times update automatically. Saved schedules work offline.

Your mosque’s Jamaat

Set a fixed Jamaat time or a delay after Azan for each prayer, with a separate sound.

Runs in the background

Enable Start with Windows and keep Azan Clock in the system tray while you work.

Getting started

  1. Save the EXE in a permanent folder and open it.
  2. Choose your city or select Get my location, then load the timetable.
  3. Use Test azan and alert to check the sound. Enter your mosque’s Jamaat times if you want separate reminders.

Keep the PC awake and signed in for alarms. This build is unsigned, so Windows may display a publisher warning.
